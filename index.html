About

I'm Abhiram R A, a student, artist, and builder from Bengaluru. I studied at Baldwin Co-Education Extension High School and now pursue humanities at Jain College — where I've gone from being a student to someone who helps run the show.

My work spans across mediums: intricate pen work in sketchbooks, vibrant digital paintings on my phone, watercolor experiments, and now — websites. I believe creativity isn't a single lane; it's everything at once.

I've coordinated inter-college fests, hosted theater events, organized school sports days, and built web tools — each project a chance to make something that didn't exist before.
